Common questions

How do I get an account?
Parents can sign up in the app with their email, Google or Apple. Your academy then links your child to your account. Coaches and admins are invited by the academy.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot Password?. Open the email on the same phone to set a new password.

Attendance didn't sync.
Attendance taken without signal is saved on the phone and uploads automatically when you're back online. Open the app once you have a connection.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the data linked to it. You can also email us to request deletion.
