Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

DesportoTrack ("the app") is operated by DesportoTrack ("we", "us") to run our football academy. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who uses the app

The app is used by parents or guardians, coaches and academy staff. Children do not have accounts. A parent or guardian holds the account, and their child's details are added by the parent or the academy.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and, optionally, phone number. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider (Apple may give us a private relay email).
  • Player details (entered by parents or the academy): child's name, date of birth, gender, playing position, the centre and squad they train with, and optional medical notes such as allergies.
  • Academy activity: training and match schedules, attendance records, coach assessments and announcements.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or browsing data, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To run the academy: scheduling sessions, taking attendance and sharing updates with parents.
  • To show each person only what their role needs. Parents see only their own children; coaches see only their squads (and never medical notes); admins see their centres.
  • To send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it only on our behalf. Coaches' phones keep a temporary copy of upcoming sessions and squad lists so attendance works offline; it is removed when they sign out.

Sharing

Information is shared only within the academy, according to each person's role, and with our service providers (Supabase for hosting; Google or Apple if you choose to sign in with them). We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep account and player information while you are part of the academy. When an account is deleted, we delete the account and the player records linked to it, except where we must keep records by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete account.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your or your child's information by emailing stridetechx@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and children cannot create accounts. Information about players under 18 is provided and controlled by their parent or guardian and the academy. Parents can review or delete it at any time as described above.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted by role at the database level. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
